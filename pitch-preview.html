Regulation marks

Ten pitches drawn by js/board-geom.js, through the real tbMarkingsHtml sliced out of app.js, styled by the real pitch rules from css/style.css. Nothing here is a mock-up.

What to check. The penalty area, goal area, centre circle and penalty spot must be the same real size on every f11 plate below, however big the pitch is. They should therefore take up more of a small pitch and less of a large one. Plates 03 and 04 are that test.

These are slightly larger than the old board, on purpose. The previous CSS used approximations: a 14% penalty area is 14.7 m where the Laws of the Game say 16.5, and the centre circle was 14.7 m across against a real 18.3. Existing boards will shift a little.
